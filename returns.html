Due to the nature of digital educational content, ALL SALES ARE FINAL. WChampFX operates a strict no-refund policy on all digital products and educational services once access has been granted or content has been delivered. This applies to:

  • Online courses where login credentials or access links have been issued
  • Live bootcamps or masterclasses once the event has commenced
  • Downloadable content once the download link has been accessed
  • Membership access once portal access has been activated
  • Bundled products where any single component has been accessed

By purchasing any WChampFX product, you explicitly acknowledge and agree that:

  • You are purchasing access to educational content, not a financial service or guaranteed financial outcome
  • Digital content cannot be returned in the way a physical product can
  • Once access is granted, the full value of the product has been made available to you
  • No refund will be issued due to personal dissatisfaction, change of mind, or failure to use the content
  • No refund will be issued due to trading losses experienced after completing or partially completing any course
Key point

The purchase of educational material does not guarantee any financial return, trading profit, or specific outcome. Trading involves significant risk. WChampFX teaches methodology only. Results depend entirely on the individual's application, discipline, risk management, and market conditions.

5.What Does Not Qualify for a Refund

The following do not constitute valid grounds for a refund under this Policy:

  • Change of mind after purchase
  • Failure to use or engage with the purchased content
  • Dissatisfaction with personal trading results following completion of the course
  • A claim that content did not meet expectations not explicitly stated in the product description
  • A claim that the course constitutes a "regulated activity" — WChampFX sells educational material only, which is not a regulated financial activity
  • Advice received from a third party that the purchase was inadvisable
  • Financial hardship arising after the date of purchase
  • Refund requests submitted after the 7-day submission window

6.Regulatory Position — Educational Content Is Not a Regulated Activity

WChampFX FZE is an educational content provider. The sale of trading education — including courses, bootcamps, and learning materials — is not a regulated activity and does not require financial services authorisation from any regulator, including but not limited to:

  • The UAE Securities and Commodities Authority (SCA)
  • The Dubai Financial Services Authority (DFSA)
  • The UK Financial Conduct Authority (FCA) under FSMA 2000
  • Any equivalent regulatory body in any other jurisdiction

Regulated activities — such as managing investments, giving personalised investment advice, dealing in financial instruments, or operating collective investment schemes — are distinct from the provision of general trading education. WChampFX does not engage in any regulated activity.
